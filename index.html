The Event

A Night of Music,
Colour & Tradition

Once a year, Jabalpur gathers around its oldest rhythm — a circle, a beat, a room moving as one. Shree Garba Beats brings that tradition back to Kachnar Club for an evening of raas and dandiya, built for a modern crowd without losing an ounce of what makes it timeless. Come dressed to move — the rest we've taken care of.
